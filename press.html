Descriptions

In one line

Students walk where they want. Pave accordingly, before the bell.

Short

Desire Lines is a strategy game for iPhone and iPad. You are the campus planner. Each week you choose what gets built and where. Between classes, students cut across the lawn by the cheapest route, and you pave where they actually walk. The bell rings every 45 seconds, and ten late students in one period ends the run.

Long

Students walk where they want. Pave accordingly, before the bell.

In Desire Lines you are the new campus planner. The Dean has a master plan; the students have feet. Each week three people on campus ask for something, a library, a science lab, a dining hall, a bus stop, and you take one and place it anywhere on the lawn. Between classes, students cut across the lawn, each taking the cheapest route. If your paving is too much of a detour, they ignore it and walk on the grass. Grass wears into a trail, then into mud, and mud is slow.

Your main tool is paving stones, and each bell pays you more for the students who were on time. Drag to lay them along the trails. Drag along a path to widen it. Press and hold, then drag, to lift paving nobody uses, and the stones come back in full. The rest of the toolbox comes from what people who manage real paths do: stepping stones, footbridges, steps, hedges, signs and a gate through a wall.

The bell rings every 45 seconds. Anyone still walking at the bell is late, and ten late in one period ends the run. When a run ends, a replay shows where they were held up and one sentence says why. Before the next bell, faint lines show where students will walk next; while you drag a path, those lines snap onto it only if students would actually use it.

A week is four periods: Arrive, Change, Lunch and Afternoon. A term is four weeks of building and an exam week. The Dean sets three goals for the term: enrol enough students, keep lateness down and keep the lawn. Meeting all three is an A. When a run ends, the student paper prints the result as a front page, and a good enough grade earns an invitation to the next campus.

There are four campuses, each with a landmark and one idea. Alder Quad is a walled quad with a clock tower. Millbrook has a stream with one ford and an old mill, and footbridges are the prize. Lakeshore puts a lake across the shortest line, beside a boathouse. Castle Hill is a slope nobody wants to climb, with an observatory.

Play a Term for a grade, keep going in Endless, take on the Daily Campus, one seeded campus a day that is the same for everyone, with a Game Center leaderboard and a share card, or open Garden: no bell, no failure and unlimited stones.

Every stone has a haptic tick, and all the sound is generated in the app. Desire Lines is free, with no in-app purchases, no ads, no accounts and no tracking.

Key features

  • Students choose their own routes. They take the cheapest way, and ignore paving that is too much of a detour.
  • The lawn wears. Grass becomes a trail, then mud, and mud is slow.
  • One finger. Drag to lay paving, drag along a path to widen it, press and hold then drag to lift it for a full refund of stones.
  • The bell. It rings every 45 seconds. Ten late in one period ends the run.
  • Honest feedback. Faint lines show the next rush before it happens, and a replay with one sentence explains a failed run.
  • Four campuses. Alder Quad, Millbrook, Lakeshore and Castle Hill, each with a landmark and one idea.
  • You build the campus. Three requests a week; take one and place it anywhere on the lawn.
  • A toolbox from real practice. Paving, stepping stones, footbridges, steps, hedges, signs and a gate, with a field guide to where each comes from.
  • The Dean's three goals. Enrolment, lateness and lawn decide the grade.
  • Four modes. Term, Endless, Daily Campus and Garden.
  • Haptics and generated sound. No licensed music.
  • Free. No in-app purchases, no ads, no accounts, no tracking. Data Not Collected.

About the developer

Denvered is an independent developer of apps and games for Apple platforms. Its apps share a few rules: no accounts, no ads, no trackers, and whatever can stay on your device does.

Using the name

The game is called Desire Lines, two words, each with a capital. The campuses in the game are invented places and are not based on any real campus.
