Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

The short version

  • Desire Lines collects no data. Its App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.
  • No accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, and no analytics, advertising or tracking software.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device.
  • Game Center is optional. It is Apple's service, and what it handles is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

1. Who we are

Desire Lines is a game for iPhone and iPad published by Denvered ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles, where it goes and the choices you have. Questions: support@denvered.com.

2. What the game handles

Progress, grades, campuses and settings
Used to let you carry on where you left off. Stored on your device only. Never sent to us or to anyone else.
Buying the game
Handled by Apple. We do not see your name, your payment details or your purchase history.
Game Center, if you use it
Leaderboards and achievements. Handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. See section 3.
The Daily Campus share card
A picture made on your device. It goes only where you choose to send it.
Beta diagnostics (TestFlight builds only)
A diagnostics file kept on the device. Never transmitted. See section 4.
Email you send us
If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include. We use it only to reply, and you can ask us to delete it.

3. Game Center

Desire Lines uses Apple's Game Center for leaderboards, including the Daily Campus leaderboard, and for achievements. Game Center is optional: the game does not need it, and you choose whether to sign in.

Game Center is run by Apple. When you are signed in, scores and achievements are sent to Apple, and other players may see your Game Center name beside your score. Apple handles that information under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your Apple Account, your email address or your contacts. You control your Game Center profile and its visibility in Settings > Game Center on your device.

4. TestFlight beta builds

Beta builds of Desire Lines, distributed through Apple's TestFlight, keep a diagnostics file on the device to help with testing. That file stays on the device and is never transmitted by the game. A tester can choose to export it and share it with us; nothing leaves the device unless they do. Builds from the App Store do not write this file.

TestFlight itself is Apple's service. If a tester chooses to send feedback or a crash report through TestFlight, Apple passes it to us under Apple's own terms, and we use it only to fix the game.

5. What we do not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in of our own.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising software from us or from third parties.
  • No tracking of you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Your progress and settings stay on your device until you reset them or delete the game. You can reset your progress in Settings in the game. Deleting the game removes everything it stored. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Scores you have posted to Game Center are held by Apple and are managed through your Game Center account.

7. Children's privacy

Desire Lines is suitable for all ages and is not directed at children in particular. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Game Center is controlled by Apple and can be limited by a parent or guardian with Screen Time. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, write to support@denvered.com and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data. Desire Lines keeps your data on your own device, so you can use these rights directly by resetting your progress or deleting the game. We do not sell or share personal information. If there is something we can help with, write to support@denvered.com.

9. This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics, no tracking, no scripts and no third-party fonts or other resources. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. For a significant change we will also say so in the game.
